Design philosophy

Reconnecting humanity with nature

Landscape design is at the heart of ART+CHORK. We believe landscape is a bridge connecting humanity with the countless forms of life with which we share our planet.

As biodiversity continues to decline, we see landscape design as an opportunity to preserve and enrich nature while creating meaningful encounters between people and the natural world.

Rather than simply creating something new, we seek to discover the inherent beauty and potential of each place, nurture its ecological richness, and manifest environments where all forms of life can flourish together.

The name

Art
The manifestation of something that cannot be put into words.
Chork
A fork and a pair of chopsticks joined into one utensil: an invention that seems useful, yet never quite works.

The chork stands for the conflict and inequity between East and West, today spoken of as the Global North and the Global South.
